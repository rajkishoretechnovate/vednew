Treatments

All Eye Care and Surgeries, Planned Under One Roof

Whatever your eye care need, our team will guide you to the right speciality and surgeon in India.

Cataract Surgery

Replace the clouded natural lens with a clear artificial lens (IOL) to restore clear vision.

Oculoplasty

Surgery for the eyelids, tear ducts and the area around the eyes, for both functional and cosmetic needs.

Glaucoma Care

Treatment & Monitoring to help protect optic nerve from damage caused by raised eye pressure.

ICL (Implantable Contact Lens)

A lens placed inside the eye, in front of the natural lens, to reduce dependence on glasses and contact lenses.

Retinal Surgeries, Injections and Lasers

Specialist care for conditions that affect retina, the light-sensing layer at the back of the eye.
